01 / Why this exists

The honest version
The purpose

Attention for the work, nothing else.

A coin as a business card. Creations of the Ill Minded is a portfolio of companies, software and clothing built by one person. A token is the loudest, cheapest distribution channel on the internet. 03 / The founder sale policy

The founder can sell.
On a schedule, announced first.

Silent founder selling is how these things end. So the rules are set before launch, borrowed from the scheduled trading plans public company executives use, and every sale is announced ahead of the window it happens in.

Rule 01

Nothing moves below the threshold

Founder tokens stay locked until the market cap clears a stated threshold. Below it, zero sales, no exceptions. Threshold: TBD.

Rule 02

Vesting, not a cliff and a dump

The 10 percent releases on a vesting schedule rather than unlocking at once. Schedule: TBD.

Rule 03

Announced before the window opens

Each sale window is published in advance with its size and its dates. No discretionary selling inside the window beyond what was announced.

Rule 04

Paced so it does not nuke the price

Sales go out in pieces sized against liquidity, not in one block. The pacing rule is published with the schedule.

None of this is a promise about price ↓

The part that matters

$ILLMINDED is not an investment.

It is a marketing campaign for an independent portfolio of work. It is not a security, not an offer to sell anything, and not financial advice. There is no business behind it generating revenue for holders, no promise of value, no expectation of profit, and no obligation on anyone to buy or support the price. The token can and very likely will go to zero.

Nothing on this page is a commitment to future performance. The founder holds an allocation and will sell portions of it under the published policy above. Assume the founder is selling into any strength. If you cannot lose whatever you spend without it mattering, do not spend it.
